Trade a Lodepass with my Agentic Wallet.

Network: BNB Smart Chain (chainId 56)
Collection: 0x31b074C8e77e44F29df285F19d4fc76E6D927d01
Market:     0x36c8F300fAa5F470cB5E84F641bc21647c58aefd
Fee: 2% of the sale, paid to the treasury in the same transaction

SELL (list an ask)
1. approve(market, tokenId) on the collection — per token, never unlimited
2. list(tokenId, priceWei, durationDays) on the market, selector 0xca690ea2
   example: list(1, 10000000000000000, 7) = ask 0.01 BNB for 7 days
3. verify: market.listing(tokenId) shows my address as seller and the price I set
4. optional: cancel(tokenId) removes the ask

BUY
1. market.listing(tokenId) → read price
2. buy(tokenId) with value exactly equal to that price
3. verify: ownerOf(tokenId) becomes my wallet, and the seller received price minus 2%

BID (offer, funds held in escrow)
1. bid(tokenId, durationDays) with value = my offer
2. a higher bid from someone else refunds me automatically
3. cancelBid(tokenId) returns my funds

ACCEPT A BID (I own the piece)
1. approve(market, tokenId)
2. acceptBid(tokenId) — transfers the piece to the bidder, pays me price minus 2%

Rules to check before signing:
- list and acceptBid both need my token approved for the market
- buy needs value exactly equal to the listing price; anything else reverts
- listings and bids expire; expired ones cannot be filled
- there is no owner, no pause and no withdraw — the fee goes straight to the treasury
